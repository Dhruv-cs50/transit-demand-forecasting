## Known issues logged (not fixed today)

- **`machine_learning_files/api.py` `horizon_hours` still has a units mismatch.**
  The fix above makes the caller's value take effect and stops it from being
  silently dropped, but `horizon_hours` (valid range 1–168 per the Pydantic
  `Field`) converts to **at most 1 forecast step** at the project's actual
  `"MS"` (month-start) cadence — 168 hours is only one week, far short of a
  full month. So every value in the documented valid range now produces the
  same "next month" forecast. That's more correct than before (which ignored
  the field entirely and always returned 6 months), but the REST API's
  hour-based interface doesn't really fit a monthly model. Worth a product
  decision: rename the field to `horizon_months` / `horizon_steps` so it
  matches what the model can actually resolve, rather than keeping the
  hourly framing as a leftover from what looks like an earlier hourly-cadence
  design.

  bundle. Both confirmed still present; still needs a product decision
  (delete vs. wire up) rather than a one-line fix.

  computes `max_ridership` in `build_ridership_json()` but never uses it;
