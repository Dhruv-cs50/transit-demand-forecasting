### New bug fixed today (previously only flagged, not confirmed)

Yesterday's log flagged, but did not fix, a suspected sibling of bug #10 in
`Processing/ablation.py`. Today's session traced the actual call graph end
to end and confirmed it as a real, separately-broken bug — worse than #10,
not just a smaller copy of it:

| # | File(s) | Bug | Severity |
|---|---------|-----|----------|
| 12 | `Processing/ablation.py` (`run_inference_with_config`) | Two compounding failures. (a) `feature_store` was fed to `predictor.predict()` in full, unsliced — `make_splits()` only *masks* train/val/test by timestamp, it never trims the underlying store, so the test period's own timestamps went in as "history," leaving AutoGluon nothing after them to forecast. (b) `known_covariates` was never passed at all, even though `finetune.py` fits this same predictor with `known_covariates_names` set — identical root cause to bug #10, but `run_inference_with_config` didn't even have `test_df` (the train/test cutoff) in scope to fix it, unlike `predict.py`. Net effect: every ablation config's `predictor.predict()` call raised (caught and logged by `run_ablation`'s try/except and silently skipped), so `python evaluation/ablation.py` produced zero rows for every config once a fine-tuned model existed — the entire covariate-impact study silently never ran, past a generic "Ablation produced no results" error log. Fixed by threading `test_df` into `run_inference_with_config`, splitting the (ablated) feature store at the test period's start into history/future, building `ts_df` from history only, and passing a `known_covariates` `TimeSeriesDataFrame` built from the future slice — mirroring `predict.py`'s `_finetuned_forecast()` pattern exactly. | High |

All 12 fixes `python3 -m py_compile`-checked; #12 additionally traced
against `finetune.py`'s `known_covariates_names` fitting and `predict.py`'s
now-fixed `_finetuned_forecast` to confirm the same AutoGluon contract
applies here.
