## Verified NOT bugs (false positives repeated across many open PRs — do not re-report)

- `.dt.tz_localize(None)` is safe on both tz-naive and tz-aware Series/Timestamps — it's `tz_convert(None)` that silently produces a *wrong value* (not a crash) when applied to a tz-aware Series whose wall-clock time matters, which is exactly bug #4 above. These are two different failure modes; don't conflate "tz_localize(None) never raises" with "tz_convert(None) is always fine."
