## Correction: two "recurring bugs" from prior logs are not actually bugs

Before today's fixes, two claims repeated across many of the 36 open PRs were
checked against real behavior instead of assumed:

- **`tz_localize(None)` "crashing on tz-aware/tz-naive Series"** — cited as a
  bug in PRs #1–#30+. Verified directly against installed pandas (3.0.3):
  `Series.dt.tz_localize(None)` and scalar `Timestamp.tz_localize(None)` are
  safe on **both** tz-naive and tz-aware values — tz-naive is a no-op,
  tz-aware strips the zone. It's `tz_convert(None)` that raises on tz-naive
  input, and every `tz_convert(None)` call site actually present in the repo
  (`machine_learning_files/merge_pipeline.py`, 3 sites) is already correctly
  guarded with a `.dt.tz is not None` check. The unconditional
  `tz_localize(None)` calls in `evaluation/validators.py`,
  `scripts/export_website_data.py`, and `models/baselines/prophet_baseline.py`
  are not bugs and don't need guards.
- **`from chronos import Chronos2Pipeline` "nonexistent import"** — cited in
  PRs #28, #29, #31, #32. Downloaded the real `chronos-forecasting` wheel
  (2.3.1, what `requirements.txt`'s `>=1.3.0` pin actually resolves to today)
  and confirmed `Chronos2Pipeline` **is** exported from `chronos.chronos2`.
  `models/chronos2/predict.py` imports it correctly; no fix needed.

Both were repeated across multiple unmerged branches without being checked
against the actual library. Flagging so future passes don't re-"fix" them a
third time.
