| # | File(s) | Bug | Severity |
|---|---------|-----|----------|
| 1 | `machine_learning_files/fetch_events.py` (`TicketmasterClient.get_events_for_venue`) | Ticketmaster returns a full tz-aware `dateTime` for most events but falls back to a date-only, tz-**naive** `localDate` for TBD-time events (real-world behavior for festivals etc.). `fetch_all()` concatenates this frame with the always-tz-aware NHL frame and calls `pd.to_datetime()` on the combined `timestamp_start` column, which raises `ValueError: Cannot mix tz-aware with tz-naive values` the moment a single TBD-time event appears anywhere in the requested range — with no surrounding `try/except`, this kills the entire run and **no events file is written at all**, even though every other event fetched successfully. Reproduced with a synthetic tz-aware Sharks row + tz-naive Ticketmaster row concatenated then passed through `pd.to_datetime`. Fixed by normalizing `ts_start` to tz-aware (`tz_localize("America/Los_Angeles")` when naive, `tz_convert` otherwise) immediately after parsing, before it ever reaches `pd.concat`. | High |
| 2 | `machine_learning_files/fetch_weather_openmeteo.py` (`_to_dataframe`) | Open-Meteo's hourly arrays can contain a documented `null` for a given hour (especially near the forecast-horizon boundary). `json.loads` turns that into `None` → pandas stores it as `NaN`, and `int(NaN)` in the `weather_code` → `weather_desc`/`is_raining` lookup raises `ValueError: cannot convert float NaN to integer`. Caught per-station by the surrounding `try/except` in the caller, so it doesn't kill the whole script, but it silently drops that station's weather for the entire requested range — a plausible root cause feeding the exact "one station's weather fetch fails silently" symptom the 2026-08-26 regional-fallback fix was written to paper over. Reproduced with a synthetic hourly payload containing one `null` `weathercode`. Fixed by guarding both lookups with `pd.notna(c)`, defaulting to `("Unknown", False)`. | Medium |
| 3 | `transit_eda/notebooks/eda_complete.py` (Section 4A, Lorenz-curve chart) | `sorted_exits` is ascending, so `cumulative[top20_idx]` (the curve's value at the 80%-of-stations mark) is the cumulative share held by the **bottom** 80% of stations — not the top 20% as the chart's axhline/annotation claimed. Reproduced with a synthetic 10-station skewed toy case: old code printed "Top 20% of stations = 8% of exits"; the true top-20% share (the two highest-volume stations) is 92% — the label asserted the opposite of reality. Purely a chart-annotation bug; the separately-computed `top5_pct`/`top10_pct` used in the Section 5 narrative text were already correct and unaffected. Fixed by labeling `100 - cumulative[top20_idx]*100` (the true top-20% share) while keeping the marker anchored at the curve's actual point. | Medium |

  `start_local` tz-naive when `start_utc.tzinfo` is falsy (mirrors the same

  to keep today's diff focused on verified-live bugs.
