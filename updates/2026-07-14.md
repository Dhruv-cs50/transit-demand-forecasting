| 2 | `machine_learning_files/merge_pipeline.py` (`add_calendar_features`) | `is_am_peak`/`is_pm_peak` were derived from `ts.dt.hour`, but this pipeline's actual granularity is monthly (`configs/model.yaml: granularity: "monthly"`), so every timestamp sits at midnight — these were always constant `False`, a dead signal fed straight into ablation, Chronos-2 fine-tuning, and zero-shot covariate lists. This meant every ablation config except `6_full_model` fed the single fixed, fully-covariate-trained `TimeSeriesPredictor` a *smaller, mismatched* schema at inference (e.g. `1_baseline` passed only `target`, none of the covariates the model was fine-tuned on) — likely to fail or produce meaningless predictions, silently caught by `run_ablation`'s blanket `except Exception: log.error(...)`, defeating the point of the ablation study for 5 of its 6 configs.
