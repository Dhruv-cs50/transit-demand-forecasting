## Bugs fixed today

Delegated three parallel, adversarial reads split by area (data ingestion +
feature engineering; modeling + evaluation; serving/website/config/docs),
each instructed to read the last several days of `updates/*.md` first and
report only genuinely new, currently-real findings. One real bug was
confirmed today (in the modeling track) by downloading and reading the actual
pinned `autogluon.timeseries==1.6.1` wheel's source directly — continuing the
project's practice of checking library-API-mismatch suspicions against real
installed/downloaded source rather than guessing:

| # | File(s) | Bug | Severity |
|---|---------|-----|----------|
| 1 | `models/chronos2/finetune.py` (`build_predictor`, `main`) | `build_predictor()`'s own docstring states step 4 of what fitting does: *"Use the val split internally to select best weights."* But the function only ever received `train_ts` — `main()` computed `val_ts` right alongside it but never passed it through — and `predictor.fit()` was called with only `train_data=train_ts` plus `num_val_windows=2, val_step_size=prediction_length`, no `tuning_data`. Downloaded and read the real `autogluon.timeseries==1.6.1` wheel's `predictor.py`: `tuning_data`'s docstring confirms it is "reserved for model selection and hyperparameter tuning... used to compute the validation scores," and that supplying it disables AutoGluon's own internal multi-window backtesting (`num_val_windows` forced to `0`) — exactly the mechanism the docstring above claims is happening. Without it, AutoGluon instead carved its own validation windows out of the tail of `train_ts` itself — a materially different, overlapping-with-training mechanism, silently defeating the project's leakage-free chronological val split for model/ensemble selection (`configs/model.yaml`'s `val_end` is exactly `prediction_length` — 6 months — long, strongly suggesting `tuning_data` was the intended design and the wiring was simply dropped). Also confirmed via the wheel's `_check_and_prepare_data_frame_for_evaluation()` that `tuning_data` must contain *more* than `prediction_length` rows per item (it slices context rows ahead of the held-out target) — so the correct fix passes `train_df + val_df` concatenated, not `val_df` alone, mirroring the `train+val` pattern `main()`'s own comment already used for `evaluate_on_val()`. This was not a crash — `evaluate_on_val()`'s reported numbers are still computed on genuinely unseen `val_df` rows — so it affected model-selection quality and the docstring's accuracy, not the final printed metrics. Fixed by adding a `tuning_ts` parameter to `build_predictor()`, building `full_ts` (train+val) once in `main()` and passing it through as `tuning_data`, and dropping the now-dead `num_val_windows`/`val_step_size` kwargs (AutoGluon ignores/overrides both once `tuning_data` is supplied). Verified by reading the downloaded wheel's exact length-guard logic against `configs/model.yaml`'s split lengths (train=24 months, val=6 months=`prediction_length`) rather than a live `fit()` run — no GPU/weights available in this sandbox, consistent with every prior AutoGluon-API finding logged since 2026-09-08. | Medium |
